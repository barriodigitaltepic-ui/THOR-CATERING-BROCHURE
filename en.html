About us
Every event,
a unique experience
At Thor Catering Events we transform special moments into unforgettable memories. With experience in the events and gastronomy industry, we offer a full-service experience that combines flawless planning, signature cuisine, and attention to every detail of your celebration.

We operate in Puerto Vallarta, Jalisco and Bahía de Banderas, Nayarit — one of Mexico's most beautiful destinations for national and international destination events.
